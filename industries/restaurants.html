A few good questions.

Can it confirm allergen information?

Your published information can form part of the setup, but guests should confirm dietary requirements with your restaurant team. Do not rely on a demo for allergen advice.

Can I keep my current phone number?

That depends on the telephone provider and routing setup. Number forwarding, availability and any costs need to be confirmed before activation. The website does not connect to your number.

Can I try it now?

Explore a written call example or create your own greeting. The telephone service is in development, and these previews do not connect a live call.

What information will you need from me?

Your services, opening hours, prices, FAQs and approved links form the starting point. Supported integrations and setup timing will be agreed before activation.
