The ElevenLabs barber AI

The barber AI on our practice page uses ElevenLabs for a live spoken conversation. It uses a saved ElevenLabs voice and AI-generated replies. It is separate from the guided browser examples below and from our future telephone service. All bookings are practice only.

The initial button checks microphone access on your device. It immediately stops the test stream and does not record or send this test audio. If access is blocked, the ElevenLabs widget is not loaded. After a successful check, the widget loads and contacts ElevenLabs to obtain the agent configuration. Starting a conversation sends microphone audio to ElevenLabs and its service providers. A notice is shown before you start. Call-audio storage is disabled for this demo; conversation transcripts are retained in the ElevenLabs account for 7 days. The widget may store a visitor identifier in your browser. Use fictional details and avoid sensitive information.

Choose End conversation in the assistant to stop. Closing the assistant, leaving this page or putting it in the background removes the widget and ends its session. Nothing automatically restarts. Demo sessions are limited to 5 minutes, with a 30-second silence timeout. Only the barber assistant is connected; other industries still use guided examples.

The temporary browser demonstration

This demo uses your browser’s speech recognition and speech playback, with predefined answers about fictional businesses. It is not the finished HelloDesk AI chatbot, and cannot make bookings, place orders or take payments.

Microphone access

Select “Start conversation” to request speech recognition. Your browser may ask for microphone permission. The “Mic on · listening” indicator follows the browser’s audio-capture event. After each reply, use “Ask another question” to start the next listening turn.

Where your audio goes

Your browser may send audio to its provider’s online speech recognition service. That provider’s processing and retention rules apply. Speech playback may also use an online voice service, depending on your browser and voice. HelloDesk does not record, upload or persist audio itself, or save the captions. Captions remain in the current page’s memory and are cleared when you end or switch the demo. Do not share sensitive information.

Ending the conversation

“End conversation” aborts recognition, cancels playback and clears captions. Switching business type, leaving the page or moving the page into the background also ends the temporary session. Nothing automatically restarts.

Without a microphone

“Hear an example” plays an introductory sample using your browser’s voice without requesting a microphone. If speech recognition is unavailable, you can still explore the industry pages or try another browser. Check device volume if playback is silent.

Booking practice

The guided examples inside the booking-practice page use a predefined flow with browser speech recognition and playback. It is not the finished AI and uses fictional sample services and slots. Confirming creates only an in-memory practice summary; it does not contact a business, reserve a calendar slot or send messages.

Voice practice starts only after you press Start voice practice. It speaks each prompt, then starts a listening turn. The microphone indicator follows browser audio events. Each capture stops before the next spoken reply. Hear prompts, use buttons does not request a microphone. End practice aborts recognition, cancels playback and clears the transcript and practice booking. Changing business type or leaving the page also clears it; switching away pauses audio and never restarts the microphone automatically. A saved practice confirmation is a text file on your device and is clearly marked as a demo.
