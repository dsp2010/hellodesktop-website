THE DETAILS THAT MATTER

Before we get started

When do I pay for receptionist setup?

The setup fee is paid before building. The subscription starts at the agreed go-live date. We agree the supported integration and test calls before handover.

What does a staff calendar mean?

A staff calendar is a separately managed appointment diary for a team member or resource. Your chosen receptionist package sets the included number; additional calendars need a supported integration.

What happens if I use more minutes or SMS?

Extra AI minutes use the rate shown on your receptionist package. Extra UK SMS costs £15 per 100 segments. SMS allowances count segments, not messages. Longer messages and some characters can use more than one segment. Usage is summarised monthly.

Is answering available outside business hours?

AI answering can operate around the clock, subject to service availability. Human support operates during agreed business hours.

How long is the website or bundle commitment?

12-month initial term, then monthly renewal with 30 days’ notice. Bundles have the same initial term because they include a website. Detailed ownership, early cancellation and source-code handover provisions are set out in the service agreement.

Does this preview book appointments or take payment?

No. The barber voice demo uses practice information, and the setup brief stays on your device. Live bookings need the agreed supported integration. Online checkout and enquiry delivery are not connected in this preview.
