Keep your attention

A ringing phone can interrupt a cut. Let’s look at the questions and requests you could get help with.

01

Fewer interruptions

  • Help answer callers while you concentrate on a cut.
  • Agree which questions need your attention.
02

Help with bookings

  • Gather appointment requests and the preferred barber.
  • Check times and book only where a connection is supported and tested.
03

Everyday answers

  • Use your services, prices and opening hours.
  • Explain your walk-in policy clearly.
04

Less chasing

  • Discuss taking messages and gathering requests.
  • Agree how those requests should reach your team.
05

Help customers remember

  • Discuss appointment reminders if available.
  • Reminders need to be agreed and tested; they cannot prevent every no-show.
06

A clearer website

  • Show services, prices and booking options.
