BEFORE YOU TRY IT

A quick heads-up.

Is this the finished chatbot?

No. This is a temporary browser demonstration with predefined sample answers, approved while our own chatbot is being prepared. It is not a general-purpose AI assistant.

Why do I press a button for each question?

Each turn starts only when you choose. The microphone is stopped before the assistant speaks, helping avoid feedback and unwanted background listening.

Why does voice not work in my browser?

Speech recognition availability depends on the browser, device, permissions and online speech service. Use Hear an example for playback without a microphone, or try another browser.

Does it book real appointments?

No. The barber shop and prices are fictional examples. Nothing is booked, ordered, paid or submitted.

Can I use my own chatbot later?

Yes. A verified assistant for your barber shop takes precedence over this temporary browser demo.
