Tests to carry out with the team

These tests do not run here. Record a result only after an agreed test of the real service. A manually entered pass is not independent verification.

1. Correct service, staff and time

Check the actual diary, appointment length, hours, breaks and requested barber.

2. No double booking

Try the same slot twice, including at the same time. Recheck availability when saving.

3. Confirmation follows calendar acceptance

Confirm only after the calendar accepts the booking. A timeout is not a confirmation.

4. Change the correct appointment

Identify the booking and verify the new time without creating duplicates.

5. Cancel the correct appointment

Check identity and cancellation rules. A retry must not cancel another appointment.

6. A clear route to the team

Test answered and unanswered transfers and where messages go.

7. No availability or connection failure

Try a full diary and a failed connection. Check the agreed next step.

8. The real telephone journey

Call the business number during an agreed test. Check routing, sound, interruptions, ending the call and any agreed texts.
