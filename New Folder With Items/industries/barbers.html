A CAREFUL START

Prove it works
for your shop.

A polished voice is part of the experience. Your actual diary, booking rules and fallback paths need to be checked before real callers rely on it.

01

The appointment reaches your diary

View the accepted booking in the actual connected calendar, with the correct service, staff member, duration and time.

02

Busy slots stay unavailable

Test staff time off, service buffers and an unavailable slot. An alternative must follow the diary’s real response.

03

Changes affect the right booking

Check identification, rescheduling and cancellation against a test appointment, including a request that should be refused.

04

Failures have a clear fallback

Test a calendar rejection, an unanswered transfer and an unknown question. A failed booking must never be described as confirmed.

These are checks to complete during setup, not claims that your booking system has already passed. Call routing, confirmation messages and caller privacy information are agreed before activation.

BEFORE WE GET STARTED

Questions a shop
owner should ask.

Can I use my existing shop number?

We review your number and provider before setup. Forwarding from an existing number is available where supported; any provider charges or restrictions must be agreed. The website demo does not connect your number.

Will it work with my booking platform?

We check the platform, supported access and the required actions before agreeing the connection. Bring the name of your current system to the setup discussion. A demo conversation does not prove that your diary integration works.

Will it know our actual prices and services?

The receptionist uses the barber shop information you supply and approve, including service names, prices, durations, opening hours and FAQs. Your setup defines what happens when information is missing or a question needs the team.

Can a customer speak to the shop?

Receptionist packages include one human transfer destination and message capture when staff are unavailable. We agree the hours and fallback behaviour during setup. Connected human transfer is an additional usage charge shown on the pricing page.

Can I make a real booking in the demo?

No. The barber voice demo uses practice information and does not create or change a real appointment. Real booking tests take place with your agreed, connected calendar before go-live.
