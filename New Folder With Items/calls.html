THE PRACTICAL QUESTIONS

Before you hand over
the phone.

The details we’ll agree for your real telephone setup.

No matching questions. Try a shorter phrase or clear the search to see every answer.

Can I keep my existing shop number?

We’ll check your provider and number before setup. Keeping your number may involve forwarding or another provider-supported arrangement. Any forwarding charges and restrictions need to be confirmed first.

What happens when my team is busy or closed?

AI answering can operate around the clock, subject to service availability. We agree whether it handles all calls, busy periods or calls outside your opening hours. Human support operates during agreed business hours.

What if the assistant does not know the answer?

We configure a fallback using your approved instructions. Receptionist packages include one human transfer destination and message capture when staff are unavailable. Unknown questions and fallback behavior are checked before go-live.

Can it transfer calls to a person?

Each receptionist package includes one human transfer destination. We agree its operating hours and unanswered-call behavior during setup. Connected human transfer time is charged separately; standard UK and other routes are explained on the pricing page.

Can it book appointments or check availability?

Receptionist packages include live availability checks and appointment booking through a supported integration. Rescheduling and cancellation use supported integrations with checks to identify the correct booking. We confirm access and test these actions before go-live. The website demo makes no real bookings.

Are calls recorded or transcribed?

The real telephone provider’s recording, transcription, access and retention settings have not been supplied yet. These arrangements and any caller notices will be explained before the service is activated.

How long does setup take?

We’ll confirm a timeframe after reviewing your barber shop information, number setup and supported integrations. There is no automatic activation or guaranteed setup deadline in this preview.

How do I update hours, prices or answers?

Essential: 1 small information-update request per month; Growth: 2 small information-update requests per month; Pro: 3 small information-update requests per month; Plus: 4 small information-update requests per month. Examples include hours, service prices and FAQs. New workflows or major changes need a separate quote. Human support operates during agreed business hours.
