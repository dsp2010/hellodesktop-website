This notice describes the current website and contact options.

Who to contact

For questions about your information or to ask for a correction or deletion, contact HelloDesk at Make an enquiry.

Enquiry drafts

The enquiry form uses your shop name, contact name, reply details and message to prepare a draft. The form itself does not send a request to HelloDesk. Your entries stay in the page and are not saved by the website.

You can review the draft before choosing email or WhatsApp. Opening either option passes the draft to that service. You choose whether to send it. Your email provider or WhatsApp then handles the message under its own privacy terms.

If you send a message, HelloDesk uses the details to respond to your enquiry, discuss your shop or help with your setup. Do not include passwords, payment-card details or customer records. No marketing subscription is created.

Examples and tools

The receptionist walkthrough and barber concept use fictional details. They make no calls, request no microphone access, take no payment and create no appointment.

The calculator runs in your browser. The setup brief stays in the page until you download a copy to your device. Neither tool sends your entries to HelloDesk. Refreshing or leaving the page can clear them.

Website access

The hosting service handles the connection information needed to serve the website. A private preview may require a ChatGPT sign-in. HelloDesk has not added advertising trackers or analytics scripts to these pages.

Before a telephone service starts

Caller notices, any recording or transcripts, who can access them and how long they are kept must be agreed before the telephone service is activated.
