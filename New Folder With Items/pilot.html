PROOF STARTS WITH A REAL TEST

A thoughtful pilot.
A clearer decision.

Use this checklist to discuss a future HelloDesk trial. Pilots are not open for registration yet, and no customer results are claimed here.

Agree what a good call looks like.

Choose a small set of everyday questions, define the answers and decide when your team needs to take over. Confirm any trial cost, duration and setup before starting.

What should we test?

Use common questions, unusual wording, interruptions, incorrect assumptions and requests the assistant cannot fulfil. Check approved answers against your current barber shop information.

What should a future case study show?

The barber shop’s permission, test dates, number of calls reviewed, how accuracy was assessed, any missed or escalated calls, and the limits of the result.

Can a pilot promise more bookings?

No. A pilot should measure what actually happens. Booking actions require a supported integration, and results depend on the barber shop and its customers.
