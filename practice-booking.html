INTERACTIVE VOICE DEMO

A good conversation.
Start to finish.

Try the everyday calls a barber shop receives. There are no fixed phrases to remember. Use fictional details throughout.

Book a skin fade

“I’d like to practise booking a skin fade on Friday afternoon.”

What to check: Checks that the shop offers the service, its appointment length and the right staff calendar before offering an available time.

Haircut and beard trim

“Can I book a haircut and a beard trim in the same visit?”

What to check: Uses the shop’s approved combined service, price and duration. It must not squeeze two services into a slot that is too short.

The customer’s preferred barber

“I’d like my usual barber. What happens if they’re not working that day?”

What to check: Keeps the customer’s preference, checks that person’s working hours and asks before switching to another barber.

When a time is unavailable

“If Friday afternoon is fully booked, can you help me find another time?”

What to check: Uses the connected diary’s real availability. If the calendar cannot respond, it explains the problem and uses the agreed fallback instead of inventing a slot.

Change or cancel an appointment

“How would you move an existing appointment, or cancel it if I cannot make it?”

What to check: Identifies the correct booking, follows the shop’s notice rules and confirms only after the supported calendar accepts the change or cancellation.

Walk-ins and waiting times

“Do I need an appointment, and can you tell me how long the walk-in wait is?”

What to check: Distinguishes the published walk-in policy from a live queue. Without a connected source, the next step is to ask the team rather than quote an invented wait.

A space opens after cancellation

“If there are no appointments, could I ask to hear about a cancellation?”

What to check: Only adds or notifies someone through a configured, supported waiting-list workflow with their agreement. The public demo does not enrol anyone or send a message.

These are prompts to say yourself, not a script sent to the AI. Changing the example does not start a call, request your microphone or connect a calendar or waiting list.

This demo uses practice information. Your real booking rules, calendar connection and telephone routing are configured and tested separately before go-live.
