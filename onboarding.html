FROM YOUR BRIEF TO GO-LIVE

Know what you’re buying.
Know what comes next.

Choose your barber receptionist, website or bundle, then agree the supported connections and scope before work starts.

01

Agree the package

Review your monthly price, setup fee, usage allowances and any additional work. Websites and bundles have a 12-month initial term, followed by monthly renewal with 30 days’ notice.

02

Pay the setup fee

The one-time setup fee is paid before building. Subscription billing starts on the agreed go-live date. Payment options will be provided when checkout is ready.

03

Build and configure

Supply your approved shop information, branding and assets. Receptionist setup includes onboarding, agent configuration, the agreed calendar connection and telephone setup.

04

Test, hand over and go live

Complete test calls and booking checks for the receptionist, or the included website revision rounds. Handover takes place before the agreed go-live date.

VAT added where applicable. Detailed ownership, early cancellation and source-code handover provisions belong in the service agreement. This website does not activate a service or verify payment.
