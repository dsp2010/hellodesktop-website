THE SHOP OWNER’S QUESTIONS

Before you hand over
the phone.

The practical details behind a receptionist that fits your barber shop.

No matching questions. Try a shorter phrase or clear the search to see every answer.

Can I keep my shop’s existing telephone number?

We review your number and provider before setup. Keeping your number may involve forwarding or another provider-supported arrangement. Any forwarding charges and restrictions are confirmed first.

What happens while we are cutting hair or the shop is closed?

AI answering can operate around the clock, subject to service availability. Human support operates during agreed business hours. We agree whether the receptionist handles all calls, busy periods or calls outside your opening hours.

Can callers choose their usual barber?

The supported booking workflow checks the requested barber, their working hours and the service they provide. It should ask before switching to another barber. The real connection is checked during setup; the public demo cannot see your staff diaries.

Can it book a fade and beard trim together?

Your approved services, prices and combined appointment lengths form part of setup. Availability checks must use enough time for the requested services and the right staff member. We test this through the agreed, supported calendar connection before go-live.

Can it change or cancel an appointment?

Receptionist packages include rescheduling and cancellation through supported integrations. The workflow must identify the correct appointment, follow your shop’s rules and confirm only after the calendar accepts the change. The website demo changes no real booking.

What about walk-ins and waiting lists?

The receptionist can explain your approved walk-in and waiting-list policy. Live queue positions, estimated waits, waiting-list enrolment and cancellation notifications need a supported, configured source and an agreed workflow. Without one, it uses your approved team contact or booking-link instructions. No waiting list is connected in this public demo.

Can a caller speak to someone in the shop?

Each receptionist package includes one human transfer destination and message capture when staff are unavailable. We agree operating hours and unanswered-call behaviour during setup. Connected human transfer is charged separately at the rate on the pricing page.

What if the diary is unavailable or the answer is unknown?

The assistant follows an agreed fallback: explain the limit, capture a message or try the approved human destination. It must not invent an available time, waiting time or booking confirmation. We test failures as well as successful bookings before go-live.

Are calls recorded or transcribed?

We agree the production telephone service’s recording, transcription, access, retention and caller notices before activation. The practice voice demo has its own settings, described on Voice & privacy.

How do I update our hours, prices or answers?

Essential: 1 small information-update request per month; Growth: 2 small information-update requests per month; Pro: 3 small information-update requests per month; Plus: 4 small information-update requests per month. Examples include opening hours, service prices and FAQs. New workflows and major changes need a separate quote. Human support operates during agreed business hours.
